Tax-free 免税

Log each tax-free receipt. Most stores need ¥5,000+ before tax (about ¥5,500 with tax) at one store on one day. Japan moved to a refund system on Nov 1, 2026: you pay the tax at the till and get it back after customs checks the goods as you leave, so keep tax-free items unused, together, and in your carry-on, and leave time for the kiosk at Haneda. Ask the store if anything's unclear.
